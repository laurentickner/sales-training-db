# The Master Sales Script — End-to-End Funnel

The complete sales call, top to bottom, in funnel order. Every line is the proven verbatim
language from the source transcripts. A rep can read this start-to-finish and run a call.

**The funnel:** Introduction → Discovery → Transition → Pitch → Committing Phase → Close →
(Objections loop back into Committing). Detail + sourcing for every line lives in
`sales-process/` and `objections/`. Raw source: `transcripts/`.

**The one rule under everything:** the pitch must be the *simultaneous explanation of why
everything they tried in the past has failed and why this is going to be different.*

---

## STAGE 1 — INTRODUCTION (rapport + frame) · ~60 seconds

**Rapport** (one or two volleys, ~30 sec — prove you're a human, not a telemarketer):
> "Hey, is this John? Hey John, it's just [name] here — what's up? ... Awesome man, I just
> got done with a workout, feeling good, ready to talk to you. You been having a good week?"

**Qualify the buying situation + take the lead:**
> "Great — well, I know we've got limited time here, so are you ready to jump in? You got a
> clean sheet of paper, something to take notes with?"

*(If they seem to have forgotten the call — lean out to pull them in: "Oh okay — gotcha,
well, is now still a good time to connect?")*

**Frame the call:**
> "Gotcha. Well John, what I've really found to work best on these calls is first just
> diving deeper into the specifics of your business and your situation — what's working,
> what's not, and what you feel are the biggest challenges keeping you from moving forward.
> Once we get clarity there, if we can help, I'm happy to walk you through what that looks
> like — and if not, I can point you in the right direction. Cool?"

**First discovery question:**
> "So, probably our best place to start is — what would you say is your biggest challenge
> right now? Or, what's not working at the level it truly could be?"

---

## STAGE 2 — DISCOVERY (information gathering) · the bulk of the call

Goal: understand IF and HOW you can help, and **eliminate objections before the close**.
Cover the 7 beliefs: pain · doubt · cost · desire · money · support · trust.

**Isolate the challenge, then probe:**
> "Tell me more. ... When you said [X], what do you mean exactly? ... How do you mean
> exactly?"

**Desired situation:**
> "What might make more sense is to begin with the end in mind — so, ultimately, what's the
> goal? ... And what's your monetary goal? ... It seems like you've thought of that number
> before — can I ask, why that number?"

**Cost / moment of decision (the two-truths):**
> "Now — what do you do for work full-time? ... Do you like it? ... What's the worst part
> about that? ... Take me back to the last day you were at work and had this realization
> that enough is enough — what happened?"

**Permission–reason why question** (for the deep personal reasons):
> "Now, can I ask you a personal question? The reason I'm asking is, what's important to me
> is not just helping you build a business that builds you wealth, but one that empowers you
> to live whatever lifestyle you want. So when you think about that — what is that for you?"

**Doubt + solution questions:**
> "What's been the biggest thing keeping you from getting there on your own? ... Have you
> tried anything like this before? Have you done coaching before?"

**Support questions** (gather, don't handle yet):
> "Are you married? Is your partner supportive of the change? Do they know you're on this
> call?"

→ Full discovery scripting + question frameworks: `sales-process/01-introduction-and-discovery.md`

## STAGE 3 — TRANSITION (get them to ask you to pitch) · ~30-60 sec

> "Look, I don't think I have any more questions — is there anything else you feel like we
> haven't covered that you feel like I need to know?"

> "Well John, based on what you told me previously, we can **definitely help**. So where do
> you think we should go from here? I can pretty much walk you through exactly how we'd help
> you get to [outcome] if you want — but you tell me where you want to go."

*(They'll ask you to pitch them. Use equal/downward inflection — never upward.)*

→ `sales-process/02-transition-and-pitch.md`

---

## STAGE 4 — PITCH (the pitch codex) · high-level promise → bridge → delivery

**Set up the pen and pad (direct command):**
> "Do you have a pen and pad? On your pen and pad, write out one through four. One, two,
> three, four." *(pause until ready)*

**High-level promise:**
> "Cool — let me preface this by saying everything we do is customized. So for you
> specifically, it's gonna be four things. Four things to help you go from [current
> situation] to [desired situation] while [removing pain / getting benefit]."

**Bridge — 4 pillars** (1 & 2 = paradigm shifts, 3 & 4 = future pacing). Each pillar:
> "When it comes to [desired outcome], here's the problem: most people out there are doing
> [incorrect behavior], and because of that they end up with [problem], which ultimately
> means [consequence]. So instead, what we do is [your method], which allows you to
> [benefit], and ultimately means [benefit of the benefit]. Does that make sense? Cool —
> what are your thoughts on that?"

*(Dialogue, not an anvil drop. Double tie-down after each big pillar. Incorporate their own
words. Back claims with proof — reason-why, your story, a case study.)*

**Delivery (~60 sec — the shortest part):**
> "So you'll be assigned an account manager, one-on-one calls, the group, the course
> material — and the reason we do it that way is so that instead of [blank] you can actually
> [benefit]."

→ `sales-process/02-transition-and-pitch.md`

## STAGE 5 — COMMITTING PHASE (temp check → onboarding → price)

**Open for questions:**
> "What questions do you have about those four things specifically — about the process
> specifically?"

*(Answer in as few words as possible. Check for a hidden objection first: "Is there a
specific reason why you're asking?" Keep the lead: "Does that make sense? Cool — what else,
what are the questions?")*

**The temp check:**
> "How do you feel about those four things we covered specifically? Do you feel like that's
> what you need to be able to get to [outcome]?"

*(Listen for tonality. "Dude, absolutely" = yes. "I think so" = no.)*

**Scale of 1-10 (if not an ecstatic yes):**
> "I hear you. What's really important to us is alignment — when you work with us we're in
> the trenches with you, our team is all in. So just to be 100% clear, on a scale of one to
> ten — one being 'I hate this guy, get me off the phone,' ten being 'that's exactly what I
> need' — where do you fall out exactly? ... What's keeping you from being a nine or a ten?"

**Onboarding before the price (paint the next cliff):**
> "So you'll process the investment with me, and once we take care of that, we set a
> baseline — homework, then your implementation pathway call where we map the next 30-60
> days. Does that make sense? Cool — and the investment is just [X]."

*(Soft, certain, downward inflection. Then go quiet — but not a weird standoff.)*

**The close (yes-ladder):**
> "How do you feel? ... Cool — you sure? ... You ready to get started on some homework? ...
> What's your billing address?"

## STAGE 6 — OBJECTIONS (loop back until 100% in, then close)

**Universal handle — diffuse, isolate, double tie-down:**
> "No problem. Just so I can stay organized — when you say [objection], are you more in a
> 'should I do this' place where you're not even 100% certain, or a 'how can I do this'
> place where you're 100% certain and it's just logistics?"

> "Money/spouse/timing aside — is there anything else keeping you from being less than 100%
> certain that this is what you need? ... So [that] aside, you're 100% in?"

**Order:** handle **uncertainty first**, always — then the logistics (money, support,
timing). A logistic handled before uncertainty is a smoke screen.

**The closes:** deposit (always give a reason), 24-hour refund, give-a-month, payment-plan
judo (open wallet → "I don't think the best thing is X, I also don't think the best thing
is nothing, so what I'd be willing to do is…"), the nervousness close, the monkey-brain
close. → `sales-process/03-committing-phase-and-closes.md`
