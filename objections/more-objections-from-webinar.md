# More Objections — from the Cole/Brian Charity Webinar

Objection overcomes that don't fit cleanly into the existing files, extracted verbatim from
the Malouf Foundation charity webinar (`[Cole-Webinar]`). Full raw transcript:
`transcripts/raw/cole-brian-malouf-charity-webinar-objections.md`.

The universal handle for ALL of these: see `00-core-framework.md`.

## "We want to discuss amongst ourselves" — 3+ business partners

> "All three equal partners — I would literally just go person by person. 'Jim, where are
> you at with this out of 10, just so I can understand? ... Got it, you're a 10. Mark,
> where are you at out of 10? ... Alex — oh, Alex, looks like you're the only one that's
> not certain. It's a two-to-three vote, looks like we're good to go.' I honestly just
> think about it as: I'm just closing three people instead of one."

> "Gotcha — so if I let you guys off and you collaborate, and you feel the same way you
> feel as you told me right now, where are we going from here? ... So when we talk
> tomorrow, after you've spoken, if you feel the same way as you told me you feel right
> now, then we're 100% moving forward — right, yes or no?"

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar]
- **Context:** In the temp check, get every partner's number. The top dog isn't always the
  one talking — a reserved "old bull" hanging back is often the decider.

## "It sounds good but I just don't know until I see it"

> "That's totally fine. Help me understand — when you say 'see it,' can you help me
> understand what you're looking to see, just so I can maybe help?"

> "Yeah, no worries — let me just share my screen right now. I'll walk you through what the
> training portal looks like, I'll give you a glimpse inside our community, so you can see
> exactly what it'll look like to be a client of ours."

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar] (Brian)
- **Context:** "I just need to see it" usually means they can't picture working with you.
  Just show them.

## "I never make same-day decisions / I always sleep on it"

> "Got it — can I tell you what I think we should do? ... Here's what I think: let's put a
> $1,000 deposit down, I'll fully onboard you into the program, and if at any moment what I
> told you we'll deliver isn't in alignment with what you're receiving, I'll give you a
> 100% refund — because we didn't uphold our end of the contract. That way it's a no-risk
> decision. Either we deliver and you have a great experience, or we don't."

> "Help me understand — if it's a no-risk decision and you're a 10 out of 10, I'm confused
> on what would hold you back from just moving forward."

> [Cole's version] "The way you make decisions before has created the results you have now
> — and those are the exact results you're trying to change, which is why you're on this
> call. So to change the results, we have to change the pattern of how you make decisions.
> ... You just told me the reason you want to do this is to make an informed decision —
> well, are you more informed from the inside or the outside?"

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar] (Brian + Cole)

## "Why should I trust you? / This feels too good to be true"

> "[Name], totally hear you. Here's what I'm gonna do — I'm gonna send you a text right
> now. ... Did you just get my text? Great — that's my personal cell phone. When I say
> this, I really care about you and your success. At any time as a client, not only will
> you have your coach, but if you ever need anything, text me or call me. I've got your
> back, I'm not going to let you fail."

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar] (Brian)
- **B2B variant:** "[Big-name client] trusted us, [client] trusted us — do you think they
  don't know how to make good buying decisions? Of course they do. You should trust this
  as well."

## "I'm not allowed to purchase any coaching program"

> "Look — what I can say is, we can definitely help. I know you said to be completely
> transparent you're not interested in any coaching program, but you just told me [blank],
> you just told me [blank] — and I know we can help you [core desired result], because we
> helped [case study, case study, case study], and we've done this ourselves. So if you're
> open, I can walk you through what the coaching program is and how it'll help you — but I
> obviously want this to be a serious conversation where you're actually considering it,
> because otherwise we're just wasting our time. So where do you want to go from here?"

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar]

## Financing not approved / weird about a deposit

> "If they applied for financing and aren't getting approved, and you can't get them down
> for a deposit — they're probably just lying about cash on hand. If they're not getting
> approved for funding, they probably don't actually have money for a deposit, but they
> don't want to tell you that."

> "Hey, no worries — if you can't put down the deposit now, are you open to just leveling
> with me for a minute on what's going through your head and what's actually going on, so
> we can put a specific game plan in place?"

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar] (Brian)

## "I'm in another contract for two more months"

> "If there are actual legal non-compete issues — nothing you can do. But if they're just
> delaying: they're signing up with you because they're not getting what they want from
> these other guys. So are you really going to wait two months and delay the process for no
> reason? You just need to isolate that delaying has an opportunity cost — if it's the
> right solution, it's the right solution today, not in two months."

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar] (Brian)

## Newer offer / "do you have clients I can speak to?" (no case studies yet)

> "When I was first selling my offer, we just leveraged the true result of the business
> owner, or of ourselves, and were very transparent: 'Yeah, we are newer — but the benefit
> is you're going to get way more attention from the actual business owner than you ever
> would anywhere else. If you join a program where the owner has thousands of clients, what
> do you think the likelihood of getting any of his time is? None.' So reframe it as an
> advantageous thing."

> "The 'no case studies' issue is usually that you fear it, so you manifest it — 80% of
> that issue is that."

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar] (Brian)

## "How do I know you're not a scammer / Bandit from Mexico?"

> "Barring that I'm a bandit who's going to take your credit card and disappear to Cancún —
> everything I said makes sense to you, you feel like it's what you need to get to [blank]?
> ... So you're just worried we're a scammer — what's going on? Here's what I'll do: come
> in, go through [the onboarding], and in the next 24 hours if you're like 'this guy's a
> scammer, he didn't fulfill on anything,' I'll just refund you."

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar]
- **Note:** On a good face-to-face Zoom call with a strong discovery, this objection almost
  never lands.

## Faith-based / "save my relationship" offers (delicate, but still close)

> "I understand that's what's really important to you — but regardless of that, what's even
> more important is you finding the truth. While I can't guarantee we'll fix your
> relationship, what I can guarantee is we'll find the truth. ... What's a hell of a lot
> scarier is for you to fast-forward five years, look back at this moment, and realize you
> could have done something and didn't — and not only lost someone close to you, but also
> lost hundreds of thousands in divorce and attorney fees."

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar]
- **Note:** Cole's point — "soft" / transformational spaces still need a closer who's *in
  the person's face*; you have to shock the person into action.

## Reframe — "these people are so sensitive"

> "If you think all your prospects are soft, overly sensitive people, you're probably being
> too rude / a bully at the end of the call — and they don't want to talk to you again. If
> you reframe your perspective — 'these people are fearful, they have a lot of fear' — and
> you're more supportive, they'll want to come back and buy."

- **Source:** [Cole-Webinar]
