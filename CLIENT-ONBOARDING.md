# Sales Call Copilot — Quick start

> 5-minute setup, then you use it live on every sales call.

---

## 1. Get in (one-time, ~30 sec)

1. Open the tool: **https://sales-training-db.pages.dev**
2. You'll see a Cloudflare "Sign in" page → type the email I added you with → click **Send me a code**
3. Check that inbox (incl. spam) for a 6-digit PIN from `noreply@notify.cloudflareaccess.com`
4. Paste the PIN — you're in for 24 hours.

If the PIN doesn't arrive in 5 min, check spam, then try again. If it still doesn't arrive, that mailbox is blocking Cloudflare — try a different email and let me know which one to allow.

## 2. Fill in **◆ My offer** (one-time, ~10 min — do this BEFORE your next call)

Top-right of the topbar → **◆ My offer**. The fields you fill in here get spliced into the Pitch + Committing scripts automatically, so the copilot talks like YOU, not like a generic template.

The 7 fields:

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **Offer name** | Just a short name, e.g. "Lean Body Method", "Agency Accelerator" |
| **Pre-frame "what this IS"** | The 2–3 sentences you say BEFORE you start your 3 pillars. Kill the "modules + good luck" / "group call with 150 people" frame, then describe how you actually deliver. |
| **Pillar 1** | Your first paradigm shift. Why their past attempts failed + your better way. End with "Are you following me so far?" (the tie-down already prints on the next line for you). |
| **Pillar 2** | Your second paradigm shift + proof — a case study, your own story, or the reason-why. End with a tie-down. |
| **Pillar 3** | The payoff. What they actually walk away with. End with a tie-down. |
| **Onboarding line (before price)** | Exact words you say to walk through what happens after they pay. Who delivers, in what format, over what timeframe. |
| **Upside-math line** | The line where you make the value concrete. Pick the right framing for your niche (next section). |
| **Price drop line** | The exact words you say at the price reveal. Soft, downward inflection. Then silence. |

Hit **Save**. Reload — your offer persists in your browser.

### Pick your upside-math framing

The dropdown lets you choose:

- **Business — client LTV × 12** — use this if you sell something that generates revenue for your client (services, ads, agency, B2B SaaS, consulting). One more client a month × 12 months = the conservative upside.
- **Fat loss / health — cost of staying stuck** — use this if you don't have a client LTV (fitness, health, lifestyle, personal development). The math is what NOT solving this has already cost them in years, energy, missed moments — and what one more year of inaction will keep costing.
- **Custom** — write any framing you want. Free text.

(The dropdown changes the example placeholder, but YOUR text in the "Upside-math line" field is what actually shows on the Committing tab.)

## 4. How to use it on a real call

Three modes. Use whichever fits the moment:

### Mode 1 — During the call (real-time)

You're on Zoom with a prospect. They just said something. You're not sure what to say back.

1. Look at the **stage strip** across the top (`1. Introduction` → … → `7. Close Confirmation`) and click the stage you're currently in. The right-hand reference panel shows exactly what to say and what to listen for.
2. Type or paste the prospect's actual words into the **left input box**. Hit Enter.
3. The right-hand panel surfaces:
   - any **objection** that was detected (with the universal 5-step handle)
   - any **discovery flag** (with the right probe to dig into)
   - if neither, a coaching note based on the stage you're in
4. On the **Discovery** tab there's a chip strip — `D · I · S · C · O · V · E · R · Y`. Click each as you cover that belief. Visual reminder of what you've installed vs what's still missing.
5. In **Live Facts** (left side, below the input) — jot the prospect's exact numbers as you hear them (revenue, leads, LTV, goal). The copilot uses these.

### Mode 2 — Before the call (Prep)

Click **◆ Prep call** in the topbar. Paste your triage notes / transcript / whatever you have on the prospect. Hit **Generate prep**. (Requires an Anthropic API key in Settings — `sk-ant-...` from console.anthropic.com. Stored only in your browser, sent only to Anthropic, costs you ~$0.02 per prep.)

You get back: what to expect, hardest beliefs to install, dig-deeper questions, an identity-frame line tailored to them, likely objections, watch-outs. Read it once before you dial in.

### Mode 3 — After the call (Review)

Click **◇ Review**. Paste the transcript (Otter, Fathom, Fireflies, your own notes — anything). Hit **Generate review**. Same Anthropic key as Prep.

You get back: per-stage adherence scores, DISCOVERY beliefs covered, voice-level moves you nailed/missed, top 3 fixes for next call, the single best next action for THIS prospect in the next 24h.

Hit **⧉ Copy review** to drop the markdown into your CRM / Notion / Slack / wherever you keep call records.

## 5. Smart mode (optional, costs ~$0.001 per analyse)

Settings → paste an Anthropic API key. Turn on **Smart mode**. Now when you analyse a prospect line, Claude reads it too and surfaces objections phrased in ways the keyword engine misses. Worth it if you can spare the cost — without it the tool still works on the keyword engine, just less fuzzy.

## 6. What it WON'T do (yet)

- Auto-attach calls to your CRM — copy + paste for now.
- Live transcription — paste from Otter/Fathom after the call.
- Mobile-friendly UI — works on tablet, best on laptop with the prospect on a second screen.
