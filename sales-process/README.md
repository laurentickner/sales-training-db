# Sales Process — Scripting Library

The full sales-call scripting from the Cole Gordon transcripts, organized by call phase.
This is the *scripting* half of the database; `objections/` is the *objection-handling* half.

Every framework here is also preserved word-for-word in `transcripts/raw/` — if anything
reads as a summary, the raw transcript has the unabridged version.

## The 6-phase sales call

1. **Introduction** — rapport + frame the call
2. **Information gathering** (discovery) — ask the questions, cover the 7 beliefs
3. **Transition** — bridge from questions to pitch with zero sales resistance
4. **Pitch** — explain the method (high-level promise → bridge → delivery)
5. **Committing phase** — temp check, get them bought in on the thesis, onboarding, price drop
6. **Objections** — see the `objections/` folder

> "We start a phase, we go across, once we check the boxes of each part we go down to the
> next phase... that way no matter where the call goes we can get things back on track." —
> [Cole-6Phases]

## The 7 buying beliefs

Every objection traces to a missing belief. All 7 are predicated on **pain** (#1).

| # | Belief | What it means |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | **Pain** | A problem OR an unfulfilled desire — the gap. Everything else is predicated on this. |
| 2 | **Doubt** | They can't fix it on their own / a proven path is faster. Kills "I'll just do it myself." |
| 3 | **Cost** | Cost of inaction > cost of investing. Answers "why now." Kills "let's circle back." |
| 4 | **Desire** | The compelling payoff of fixing the problem. The "heaven island." |
| 5 | **Money** | Resources AND willingness. (Lack of willingness is really a lack of cost.) |
| 6 | **Support** | Spouse / partner / board / team / implementer backs the decision. |
| 7 | **Trust** | Trust in you, the company, and — most important — the **method**. |

> "Our whole sales process is using the right skilled questions to break down the seven
> limiting beliefs and reinstall the empowering beliefs before we transition to the close —
> so the prospect closes themselves and views you as a leader, not a salesperson." —
> [Cole-7Step]

## The one principle under everything

> "The pitch / your solution must be the **simultaneous explanation of why everything they
> tried in the past has failed and why this is going to be different**." — repeated in
> [Cole-Packaging], [Cole-7Step], [Cole-PitchCodex] and every other source.

> "It's not about the questions you ask — it's about the answers you get." — [Cole-Pt2]
