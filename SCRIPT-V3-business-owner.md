# Scale Systems Sales Call Script V3 — business-owner ICP

Cole Gordon + Ravi Abuvala methodology, tuned for the Scale Systems ideal client:
established business owners with real revenue. This is the backup text version of
what the Call Copilot app runs. The app carries the full verbatim rebuilds for all
29 objections; this script is the spine.

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION — rapport + frame

"Hey, is this [name]? Hey [name], it's [you] here, what's up?"

"Awesome, I just got done with a workout, super excited to chat. You been having a
good week? ... Oh, is that a good busy or a bad busy?"

(If distracted / driving:) "Oh okay, gotcha, well is now still a good time to
connect? We can find a better slot if not."

"Great, I know we've got limited time, so are you ready to jump in? You got a clean
sheet of paper, something to take notes with?"

FRAME: "What I've found works best on these calls is first diving into the specifics
of your situation, what's working, what's not, and the biggest challenges keeping you
from moving forward. Once we get clarity, if we can help I'll walk you through it; if
not, I'll refer you to somebody I know or just give you some homework to work on in
the meantime. Sound fair?"

Advance when: they have paper / can focus AND said yes to the frame.

---

## 2. DISCOVERY — business-owner version (cover all 7 beliefs)

"What's your biggest challenge right now? Or what's not working at the level it truly
could be?"

"Tell me more, when you said [X], what do you mean exactly? Why do you say that
though?"

"Tell me about the business, what do you sell, who's it for, and how long have you
been running it? ... What part of running it drains you the most right now?"

"Take me back to the moment you realised the way you'd been growing this wasn't going
to get you where you want. What was going on in the business that made you decide
enough is enough?"

CHUNK DOWN TO NUMBERS (Ravi): "Last week specifically, how many leads / sales calls
did you generate? What about the week before? Out of those, how many fit the exact
ideal client you want? ... Just for clarity, what was your exact revenue last month?
What about the month before?"

Assumption-correction: "So you're making [guess] a month right now then, correct?"
(Make an assumption so they correct you with the truth.)

"Begin with the end in mind, ultimately where do you want the business to be? What's
the revenue number you're driving toward, and what would the business look like at
that point? Why that number specifically?"

Cost of inaction: "Can I ask another personal question, and I really hate to ask
this, but what's your plan if nothing changes? What if the last five years are like
the next five years?"

"I guess that makes sense, is there any other reason this is important to you now
though?"

Lifestyle / desire: "Can I ask a personal question? The reason I'm asking is we want
this to work around your lifestyle goals, what are the personal goals you want your
business to allow you to achieve?"

Past attempt / doubt: "What else have you tried to fix this? What did you like or
dislike about it? What we've found is when people don't get the result, it's one of
two things, the method or the execution. Which do you think it was?"

Support: "Are you married? Is your partner supportive of the change? Do they know
you're on this call, and what would they think if they knew?"

The 7 beliefs to land before you advance: Pain, Doubt, Cost, Desire, Money, Support,
Trust.

---

## 3. TRANSITION

"I don't think I have any more questions, is there anything else you feel like we
haven't covered that you feel like I need to know?"

"Based on what you've told me, we can definitely help."

HAMBURGER: "So where do you think we should go from here? I can walk you through
exactly how we'd get you from [current] to [goal] if you want, but you tell me where
you want to go." (Equal or downward inflection, never upward. Let them ask to be
pitched.)

---

## 4. PITCH — sell the method, 3 pillars

Pre-pitch: "Honestly, it's not your fault it hasn't worked. There's so much
complicated, conflicting information out there. For you specifically, you were just
missing a few key pieces to get to [goal]. Do you have a pen and paper? I want to
break those down."

High-level promise: "Everything we do is customised to you. For you specifically it's
going to be three things, to take you from [posting with no real revenue] to [their
goal], while [removing the pain they named]."

PILLAR 1 — Messaging / Sell the Vacation: the Japan-restaurant analogy (a great
restaurant whose menu is only in Japanese won't sell to you, not because it's not
good, but because the value isn't communicated in a language you understand). "The
first thing we do is give you the right social media strategy so your value is
communicated in a language the right people understand." Then: "Are you following me
so far? What questions do you have about that part specifically?"

PILLAR 2 — Content Strategy & System: the gym analogy (a treadmill alone won't
transform you, you need the strategy AND the system). "We build you the AI Content
System that writes your content in your voice, so it's done." Case study: someone who
joined last week booked 3 sales calls within 24 hours. Then: "What do you feel that
would do for your business? Does that make sense?"

PILLAR 3 — The Social Media Bridge: the bridge analogy (traffic on one side, your
solution on the other, no bridge means no sales). "The last thing we do is give you
the bridge, so you're left with prequalified prospects ready to buy." Then: "Does
that make sense?"

Delivery (~60 sec, last): "Here's how we work together: Lauren builds your strategy,
Mariana delivers it 1:1, plus the AI Content System and ongoing support. The reason
we do it that way is so that instead of [blank] you can actually [benefit], so you
can [benefit of the benefit]."

Tie-down after every pillar. Dialogue, never an anvil drop.

---

## 5. COMMITTING PHASE

"What questions do you have about those three things specifically?" (Answer in 1-2
sentences. Check for hidden objections: "is there a specific reason you're asking?")

CONSERVATIVE UPSIDE MATH (Ravi, before the temp check): "Quick bit of math so this
isn't abstract. You told me a client is worth about [LTV] to you. If this system just
gets you one more client a month from organic, content you otherwise wouldn't even
have posted, that's [LTV x 12] in extra revenue over the next 12 months. And that's
the conservative version, just one more client a month."

Temp check: "How do you feel about the process specifically? Do you feel like that's
what you need to get to [outcome]?"

Scale: "What's really important to us is alignment, our team is all in. On a scale of
1 to 10, one being 'I hate this guy, get me off the phone,' ten being 'that's exactly
what I need,' where do you fall exactly? ... What's keeping you from a 9 or 10?"
(Nothing in life is a 10, it's not even 100% certain you'll wake up tomorrow. A 9 is
about as close to certain as you'll ever be. And clarify: "I'm not asking if you're a
10 on the price, in terms of the three things, do you feel in alignment?")

Mini-transition: "So you feel really good about the process, no questions, what's
next, where do you want to go from here?"

ONBOARDING (before price): "Basically, next steps, you'll process the investment with
me. Once we take care of that, we set up an onboarding call, give you some homework,
and lay out when we're going to meet, how often, what we'll give you and what we need
from you, so you have complete clarity and certainty on exactly what's going to
happen over the next 12 weeks. Does that sound good?"

Price drop: "And the investment is just [X]." (Soft, downward inflection, price ends
the sentence on a period. Then go quiet.)

Close: "How do you feel? ... Cool, you sure? ... You ready to get started on some
homework? ... What's your billing address?"

---

## 6. OBJECTIONS — universal handle, uncertainty first

For every objection, run this before the specific rebuttal:

1. Diffuse: "No problem. Not an issue at all. No problem." (Triple. Pause.)
2. Isolate: "Just so I can stay organized, when you say [objection], are you more in
   a 'should I do this' place where you're not 100% certain, or a 'how can I do this'
   place where it's just logistics?" (Financial objections isolate on
   value-vs-budget instead.)
3. Temp check: "[Objection] aside, do you feel this is 100% what you need to get to
   [outcome], that it's 100% going to work, and that now is 100% the right time?"
4. Scale if needed (1-10, what's keeping you from a 9 or 10).
5. Double tie-down: "So [objection] aside, is there anything else keeping you from
   being less than 100% certain this is the right thing and now is the right time?
   ... So you're 100% in?"

Order: handle uncertainty FIRST, then logistics (timing, then financial, then
support). A logistic handled while uncertainty is unresolved is a smoke screen.

Concession rule: never give a payment plan, extension or deposit for free. Trade it:
"If I'm willing to do that for you, are you willing to move forward right now?" Going
lower again? Also ask for a case study AND a commitment to do the work.

---

## 7. CLOSE CONFIRMATION — button down the sale

"Awesome, congratulations, this is a big move and I'm genuinely excited for you.
Here's exactly what happens next: [restate onboarding steps + first call date]."

Pre-frame remorse: "Real quick, right after a big decision like this your brain might
get loud and try to second-guess it. That's normal, it's the monkey brain doing its
job. It doesn't mean it was the wrong call, it means it mattered. If anything comes
up, you call me directly, okay?"

"Let's get your onboarding form done right now while we're together, and get your
first call on the calendar. Momentum is what makes this work."

The deal isn't safe until payment clears and onboarding starts.
