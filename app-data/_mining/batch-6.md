- "You must command honesty." (the vibe: "we are just not going to BS each other on this call") — source: 85 Minutes of Sales Training That Will Make You Rich
- Three levels of rapport: seeking rapport (upward-inflecting — avoid entirely), equal/neutral (on the same side of the table — 80-90% of the call), breaking rapport (downward-inflecting — 5-10%, used when challenging or stating a key result). — source: Subcommunication & Tonality
