## What got skipped or went wrong

- **Committing phase compressed away (4/10).** No temp check, no 1-10 scale at
  all, no onboarding-before-price framing. Jumped from the upside math straight
  to "$25k ... the investment is just $7,800" with no downward-inflection pause.
- **Objections not run through the universal handle (3/10).** Jason asked for a
  payment plan twice — conceded for free, no diffuse, no isolate, no "if I do
  that, are you willing to move forward right now?" "What if this doesn't work?"
  (a pure uncertainty objection) was answered logistically instead of isolated +
  temp-checked. He self-closed it — luck, not method.
- No close-confirmation or buyer's-remorse pre-frame. Jason is a financially
  stretched buyer (driving Uber to cover bills, marital strain) — highest
  cancellation risk, left exposed.
- Money belief never installed — never confirmed he could comfortably invest,
  which is why the payment-plan scramble happened live.

## Top 3 fixes for the next call

1. Run the full committing phase every time: temp check -> 1-10 scale -> "what's
   keeping you from a 9?" -> onboarding-before-price -> then price on a downward
   inflection, then silence.
2. Trade every concession: payment-plan request -> "if I can make that work, are
   you ready to move forward right now?"
3. Handle uncertainty before logistics, and always close-confirm — especially
   the buyer's-remorse pre-frame for a stretched buyer.
