## What was run well

1. **Numbers extraction was surgical.** Lauren got: last one-on-one client = last year; LTV $8,200; zero leads last week (then corrected to one group client from networking); 20 years in business; driving Uber; wife is breadwinner; marital problems from revenue inconsistency. Quote: *"So you've only had one one-on-one client come through this year… and the rest are all the small group, right?"* This created the pain anchor.

2. **Cost-of-inaction was visceral.** Lauren looped: *"If this continues another 24 months… what's your plan?"* Jason: *"I don't know… my wife's work has gotten really tough… part of me feels like I absolutely need this to work."* Then: *"Does she know the extent of the lack of leads?"* → *"She doesn't know actual numbers."* This was identity-level exposure (20-year coach hiding revenue from spouse).

3. **Pitch structure was clean** — three pillars (strategy, AI content system, lead-conversion training), each with a mini tie-down. Example: *"So are you following me so far?"* → *"Yes."* Then: *"What questions do you have about that part specifically?"* Forced micro-commits before moving forward.

4. **Price hold under anchor mismatch.** Mariana pre-anchored $4–6k; Jason asked for that range at 55:46. Lauren: *"Right now it's not going to be possible to be in that range."* No justification, no apology. Silence. Jason: *"Okay… all right."* Then moved to payment logistics. This was a FOR-him-not-TO-him frame — she didn't defend the delta.

5. **Outcome was a full close with logistics handled live.** PayPal invoice sent on-call, payment processed, onboarding form link dropped in chat + LinkedIn DM as backup, strategy call pre-booked for Friday (then adjusted to tomorrow). No post-call "think it over."

## What got skipped or went wrong

1. **No Introduction stage.** Call opened cold: *"Tell me about your day… I just woke up… where are you located?"* No agenda-set, no outcome pre-frame, no permission for discovery. Lauren asked *"What do you want to have happen by the end of this meeting?"* at 10:31 but didn't loop it back or get a verbal commit to the process. **Cost:** No sale-won-at-hello moment; Jason stayed in "hear more about the program" mode (his words) rather than "I'm here to solve this problem" mode.

2. **Committing phase was skipped entirely.** No temp-check (*"On a scale of 1–10…"*), no *"What would make it a 10?"*, no onboarding-before-price, no silence after the ask. Lauren went straight from pitch to: *"If that's something I can do for you, is that something you'd move forward with me here now?"* (54:46). Jason immediately raised the $4–6k anchor mismatch. **Cost:** No buy-in measurement before price = objection became about money instead of fit. Caps score at 3.

3. **"What if this doesn't work?" objection was handled with humor, not the universal handle.** Jason asked at 1:07:20. Lauren: *"You're worried I'll run away with your money, steal your house, your nice office chair…"* (joke). Then: *"The reasons it wouldn't work would be if you don't post the content… or don't collaborate with us."* This was a features-answer to an uncertainty objection. **Missing:** Diffuse (*"I appreciate you asking"*), isolate (*"Other than that, is there anything else?"*), temp-check (*"If we solve that, where are you?"*), scale, double tie-down. She never got a number. **Cost:** Jason stayed in maybe-land; close only happened because of payment-plan logistics momentum.

4. **No VERIFY belief.** Lauren never asked: *"Have you tried to fix this yourself? What happened?"* She got COST (marital problems, Uber driving, $10k+ spent on Russ Ruffino, LinkedIn vendors, etc.) but didn't loop back to extract the lesson Jason learned from those failures. **Cost:** Missed chance to pre-empt "what if this doesn't work?" by having Jason articulate why past stuff failed (no strategy) vs. why this will work (strategy-first). Score drop: –1.

5. **No negative-identity flip or re-meaning.** Lauren got Jason to admit he's been "surviving" for 20 years, hiding numbers from his wife, sacrificing sleep for Uber. But she never reframed that as: *"So you've been the coach who helps clients with time management and scaling… but you've been trading time for money and can't scale yourself. Do you see the gap?"* **Cost:** No mask-off moment. Jason stayed in "I'm pretty good at this" self-concept (quote: *"I help my clients with marketing messaging… I understand the psychology"*). He closed, but didn't have an identity-shift that would prevent future churn.

6. **Logistics-answer-to-uncertainty.** After *"What if this doesn't work?"* Lauren went to: *"You'll collaborate with us… post the content… give us feedback."* This was a how-answer to a why-question. She should have said: *"Jason, you've spent 20 years trying to figure this out. You've hired vendors, bought programs, none of it worked. What's different now is you're not guessing anymore — I'm building the strategy, my team is building the system, and Daniel is making sure it works. The only way this doesn't work is if you decide not to use it. Does that make sense?"* Then temp-check. **Cost:** Caps objection-handling at 5.

## Objections that surfaced

1. **Price anchor mismatch** (55:46): *"Mariana said $4–6k… would it be possible to be in that range?"*
   - **Handle:** Lauren: *"Right now it's not going to be possible."* Silence. Jason: *"Okay."*
   - **What was missed:** No diffuse (*"I appreciate Mariana giving you a range"*), no isolate (*"Other than price, is there anything else?"*), no temp-check (*"If price wasn't a factor, where would you be?"*). She just said no and moved on.
   - **What she should have said:** *"I appreciate Mariana giving you that range — she was working off limited info. Now that we've gone deep, I can see this is a strategy + build + training play, not just coaching. If price wasn't the issue, are you ready to move forward?"* → Get a number → *"Great, so it's just the $1,800 delta. What if I can solve that?"* → Offer PayPal split as a trade (not a gift).

2. **"What if this doesn't work?"** (1:07:20)
   - **Handle:** Joke + features-answer (*"If you don't post… if you don't collaborate…"*).
   - **What was missed:** Entire universal handle. No diffuse, isolate, temp-check, scale, double tie-down.
   - **What she should have said:** *"Jason, I appreciate you asking that — it means you're taking this seriously. Other than that concern, is there anything else stopping you?"* [Isolate] *"Okay. If I can show you why this is different from everything you've tried, where would you be on moving forward?"* [Temp-check] *"On a scale of 1–10, how confident are you that if you post the content we build and give us feedback, this will work?"* [Scale] *"What would make it a 10?"* [Loop] *"So if we make sure Daniel checks in weekly and you commit to posting 3x/week, you're at a 10?"* [Double tie-down] *"Great — let's do it."*

3. **Payment logistics** (1:04:51): *"Are there payment options or just lump sum?"*
   - **Handle:** Lauren: *"Most clients pay in full… what would you need?"* Jason: *"PayPal 6-month split."* Lauren: *"Let me check… okay, let's try."*
   - **What was missed:** No trade. She gave the concession (PayPal split) for free. Should have been: *"I can do PayPal if you can get the form done in the next 2 hours and book the strategy call for tomorrow. Deal?"* Trade every concession.

## Top 3 fixes for the next call

1. **Run the Committing phase.** After pitch, BEFORE price: *"On a scale of 1–10, how confident are you this would solve your client-acquisition problem?"* → *"What would make it a 10?"* → Handle gaps → *"So if we solve X, you're at a 10?"* → *"Great. Let me show you how we'd onboard you."* → Walk through onboarding → *"How does that sound?"* → THEN price. This prevents price from being the first objection.

2. **Lock VERIFY + OWN beliefs in discovery.** When prospect lists past failures, ask: *"What did you learn from those?"* → *"So you've spent $10k+ and 20 years trying to solve this. What's the common thread?"* → Let them say *"Me / my strategy / my execution."* → *"Right. So if we take strategy off your plate and build the system for you, what happens?"* This pre-empts *"What if this doesn't work?"* by making them own the gap.

3. **Trade every concession.** PayPal split-pay, case-study discount, bonus membership access — these were all given for free. Next call: *"I can do PayPal if you can commit to posting 5x/week for 90 days and doing the case-study video. Deal?"* Make them earn it. This also raises perceived value (if it's easy to get, it's worth less).
