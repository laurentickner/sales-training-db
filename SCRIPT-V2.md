⭐ = new or upgraded vs. the original script. Everything unmarked is kept as-is because it

## 2. DISCOVERY *(budget ~20 min)*

**Clarify why they're there** — if they answered the outcome question with a *problem*,
ask about the challenge first; if a *goal*, ask about goals first. Just swap the order.

**Get the deep, personal reason**
> Seeing as we're all about driving revenue through organic social, what's important to
> you about [what they said they want]?
> · Why? · Why now though? · And seeing as you're the founder, why is [X] important to
> you… personally?

**Biggest challenge**
> What's your biggest chaaaallenge when it comes to driving revenue through organic social
> media? Or, what's not working at the level it truly could be?

**Probing** *(pick the ones that get the deep challenge out, don't ask all)*
> · When you say ____, what do you mean exactly? · Tell me more… · How long has that been
> going on? · What impact has that had on you… personally? · Is that important for you to
> change? Why? Why now though? · If that played out for another 12 months, what would that
> be like? · What else is affected because of ____?

⭐ **Moment of decision** *(gap-fix #3)*
> Take me back to the moment you decided this had to change. What actually happened? What
> was going on that day, or that week?

**What's in the way**
> What do you think has been the biggest thing keeping you from [X] until now? What's… in
> the way?

⭐ **The numbers diagnostic** *($30M video, thing #2)*
> Before I tell you what I think the fix is, I want the real picture, the actual numbers,
> so my read is accurate and not a guess. Mind running through a few with me?

Collect:
- Current monthly revenue — and how consistent is it?
- What's a client worth to you — average sale / lifetime value?
- Where do your leads come from right now, and what's each channel producing? Running ads?
  What's the monthly ad spend?
- Close rate on your sales calls?
- On social today — followers, weekly views/reach, posting frequency, and how many leads or
  booked calls is social actually producing?
- How long have you been stuck at this level?

> *Why this matters:* an accurate diagnostic only lands if it's built on real numbers.
> Guessing the constraint from three sentences kills your credibility, and you can't run
> the upside math later without them.

**Label the pain**
> Got it, so what I'm hearing is ____. Does that sound about right?

**The goal (numbers)**
> Seeing as we can't aim without a target, where do you want to get this to? Revenue-wise?
> · You seem like you've thought of that number before, can I ask, why that number?
> · When do you want to hit it by? · And where are you at right now? · How much of that is
> profit? · So we've got a [$X]/month gap. That sounds like a problem worth solving.

⭐ **Cost of inaction** *($30M video, thing #3 — upgrades the old "12 months" question)*
> One more thing before I pull this together. If we roll this forward 90 days, and you're
> still posting content that doesn't convert, still at this same revenue, dealing with this
> same bottleneck… what changes for you? What gets harder, or stays off the table?

Then go deeper, don't stop at the surface answer:
> · What do you mean by that? · And why does that one really bother you? · What would that
> feel like?

**Label the goal**
> Beautiful, I see your vision clearly. You want to ____. Love it.
> Now, can I ask a personal question? The reason I'm asking is we want this system to work
> around your lifestyle goals, so what are the personal goals you want your business to
> allow you to achieve?

⭐ **Past pain** *(upgraded with the duplicate question)*
> What else have you looked at to try to fix this? What did you like or dislike about it?
> And what we've found, doing this with a lot of founders, is when content doesn't drive
> revenue it usually comes down to one of two things, either the strategy was wrong, or the
> strategy was fine but it was never executed consistently. Which was it for you?

**Obstacle Busting** *(kept in full — this section is strong)*
Tick off every potential objection before you transition. Ask any/all relevant questions:
- **Partner** — Do they know about this? How do they feel? Are you married? What would they
  say if they were here? Should they be on this call?
- **Doubts in themselves** — Are there any business or personal reasons you believe this
  might not work for you?
- **Doubts in us** — What would you need to see from a company like ours to know this is
  right? How confident are you in us to deliver, from what you've seen so far?
- **Why us** — Why us? What made you reach out to us specifically? What do you like about
  how we do things vs. whatever else you've looked into?
- **Money** — How does investing in your business, given what you've shared, make you feel?
  If you knew we could guarantee an ROI, would that change how much you could invest? How
  much have you set aside to invest into [outcome]?
- **Platform risk** — What platforms are you using? Are you running ads? *(When you buy
  cheap, you buy twice.)*

*Looping through obstacles:* acknowledge → ask an open question → get the real issue → use
an analogy → get their confirmation they've changed their mind. Don't transition until
they're overcome.

## 6. COMMITTING PHASE

⭐ **Conservative upside math** *($30M video, thing #4 — do this before the temp check)*
> Quick bit of math so this isn't abstract. You told me a client is worth about [LTV] to
> you. If this system just gets you one more client a month from organic, content you
> otherwise wouldn't even have posted, that's [LTV × 12] in extra revenue over the next 12
> months. And that's the conservative version, just one more client a month.

**Temp check (soft)**
> Just curious, in terms of the process specifically… how do you feel?
>
> [uncertain tonality →] Gotcha, and just to be totally clear, what's really important to
> us is alignment. When you come in and work with us, we're rolling up the sleeves and
> getting in the trenches with you, our team is all in. So it's really important to us that
> you feel good about the process. You know what I mean?

**Temp check (scale)**
> With that said, how confident are you, on a scale of 1 to 10, that this is the right
> thing for you and your business, and that now's the right time? 1 being not a fit at all,
> 10 being this is exactly what I need. And we don't do 7s around here, 7's a cop-out.

- **8 or under** → "Wow, that's lower than I expected, why so low?" → handle the obstacle →
  loop → ask the number again → "So if you knew ____, would that change things?"
- **9 or 10** → "Great, so is there anything keeping you less than 100% certain that this
  is the right thing for you, and that now's the right time?" → handle.

**Onboarding**
> Great, you've come in at a great time. We used to do all of this as coaching over a
> 3-month period, 90 days to get the system running. Now, because we've built an internal
> execution team, we build it for you. Would that be valuable?
>
> We'll be charging $25,000 for this going forward. But we need 3 case studies, and you'd
> be a great fit, so if I could get you in before we go to full price, is that something
> you'd be open to?
>
> Here's how it works. Step one, I build your strategy, then you and I jump on a call to
> walk through it, that takes me 2-3 days. Step two, our team builds your AI Content System
> for you, done in a day. Does that make sense? Any questions on that?

⭐ **Re-run the upside math right before the price** *($30M video — the bonus tip)*
> So just to ground this back in numbers, if this gets you even one more client a month,
> that's [LTV × 12] over the year. And earlier you said if nothing changed in the next 90
> days you'd still be stuck with [their bottleneck]. This changes that.

**Price drop**
> The investment is $25,000 going forward, but coming in now, as one of our case studies,
> it's just $5,000.
>
> *<PAUSE — 4 seconds. Soft, certain, downward inflection. Let them speak first.>*

⭐ **The close** *(gap-fix #6 — softer yes-ladder instead of "16-digit card number")*
> How do you feel? … Cool, you sure? … Great, you ready to get started? All I'll need is
> your billing address to get you set up, and then we'll get the card sorted.

## 7. ⭐ OBJECTION HANDLING *(gap-fix #1 — entire section is new)*

Expect 1-2 objections after the price drop. That's normal. Handle them, then close.

**Universal handle, for ANY objection:**
> "No problem." *[diffuse]*
> "Just so I can stay organised on my end, when you say [their objection], are you more in
> a 'should I do this' place where you're not even 100% certain this is what you want, or a
> 'how can I do this' place where you're 100% certain and it's just logistics?"

**Then isolate + double tie-down:**
> "[Money / your partner / timing] aside, is there anything else keeping you from being
> less than 100% certain this is the right thing and now's the right time? … So [that]
> aside, you're 100% in?"

**Iron rule: handle UNCERTAINTY before any logistic.** A money or partner objection handled
while they're secretly unsure is a smoke screen, and you'll lose the deal.

**"It's too expensive / I can't afford it":**
Confirm money aside they're 100% in → "Most clients do it up front, but for certain clients
where they really want it and it's just the money, we break it up. Are you open to an
honest conversation financially so we can see if there's a way to make this work?" → run
the open wallet (what's coming in over the next 30 days → cash on hand exactly → credit
available) → "I don't think the best thing for you is to drain your savings, but I also
don't think the best thing is to do nothing. So what I'd be willing to do is [half / a
third] down, and if I'm willing to do that for you, are you willing to move forward right
now?"

**"I need to think about it":**
"No problem, before we get into that, how do you feel? Do you feel like this is 100% what
you need to hit [goal]?" → if uncertain, that's the real objection, handle it → if genuine
sleep-on-it: "Totally fine, so if we schedule a time tomorrow and you wake up feeling the
same exact way, that means we're 100% moving forward, yes?"

**"I need to talk to my partner":**
"No problem, for you personally, do you 100% want to do this? … If it was solely your
call, we'd be moving forward? … Is this you making the decision and just letting them know,
or do you genuinely need to decide together?" → if collaboration: refundable deposit now +
get the partner on a call with you so they see exactly what you'd be investing in.
*(Decision-makers should already be confirmed from the triage call — if a new one surfaces
here, that's a flag the triage missed it.)*

**Competitor cheaper / nerves / "want to see it" / other:** full rebuttals in the
`objections/` folder.

**Payment plan:** if they ask, don't let them set the terms. Run the open wallet first,
find the biggest plan they can comfortably do, then trade it: "If I'm willing to do that
for you, are you willing to move forward right now?"
