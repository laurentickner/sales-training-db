Step 5 of 7 · what are pillars?

What are pillars, and why do we use them?

When you're on a sales call, the pitch isn't a list of features ("you get 12 calls + Voxer + workbook"). Features get compared on price. The pitches that land are built on 3 pillars, each carrying an analogy that helps your prospect finally see why what they've been doing hasn't worked, and why your way changes everything for them.

1
Pillar 1, the mechanism shift
Names the wrong model your prospect is operating on, and the right one. e.g. "you think weight loss is willpower, it's actually hormonal calibration."
2
Pillar 2, the method, with proof
Your specific approach plus a real client win that demonstrates it works. e.g. "Sarah came in at X, we did Y, she ended at Z in 90 days."
3
Pillar 3, the future they walk into
Who they become once this works. The identity shift, the version of themselves that lives on the other side of your program.

We've taken what you wrote and turned it into your version of these 3 pillars. Review them on the next screen, edit anything that doesn't feel like you, then we open the tool.

Step 7 of 7 · quick tour

Here's how you'll actually use the tool.

3 things to know before your first call. Once you click below, we'll guide you through them in the live tool.

📜 The Stage script (left, big panel)
Read this line by line on your call. Tick each line as you say it. That's the call. There are 7 stages, the tool walks you through them in order.
📝 The Call log (right side)
When the prospect says something you didn't expect, type their exact words into the box and hit Enter. The tool surfaces the next move ("your prospect is hedging, here's the script for that").
🆘 The HELP button (top, red)
If a call goes sideways, hit HELP. The tool pauses, surfaces 3 buy-yourself-time lines you read verbatim, then helps you get back on track.
▶ Start call (top, green)
When you're actually on a call, hit this. The tool collapses into a focused mid-call view, only the live script, call log, and HELP are visible. No Prep / Settings / My offer clutter. Hit End call when you're done to get the full toolkit back.
